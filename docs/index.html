Privacy Policy for Color It

Effective Date: June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Imstrk handles information collected from users of our mobile application, Color It.

1. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Color It is a children's game designed for users under the age of 13. We are fully committed to protecting the privacy of children. We do not knowingly collect, request, or store any personal information from children. Our App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data directly from you. The App stores drawings locally on your device, and no personal data is transmitted to our servers.

3. Third-Party Service Providers (Advertising)

To support the App, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and use non-personally identifiable information, such as advertising IDs and diagnostics, to serve ads. We have configured Google AdMob to run under child-directed settings globally, which means:

  • No personalized ads are served.
  • No behavioral tracking or profile building occurs.
  • Ads are restricted strictly to General Audience (G-rated) content suitable for children.

4. Permissions

The App requests standard system permissions only when necessary for local functionality:

  • Storage / Media: Required only to save drawing creations to the system gallery on your device.
  • Network / Internet: Required to request and display G-rated ads from Google AdMob.
  • Ad ID: Standard identifier used by Google AdMob for children-compliant advertising delivery.

5. Security

We value your trust. Since we do not collect or transmit personal information over the internet, your children's data remains private on your local device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
